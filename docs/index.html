Circuit Firmware Uploader

Runs in your browser. Nothing to install.

Before you start

  • Close Novation Components and any music software. Windows will not let two programs use the Circuit at once — this is the most common cause of failure.
  • Connect the Circuit by USB directly to the computer, not through a hub.
  • Put it in bootloader mode: turn it off, then hold Scales + Note + Velocity while turning it on.
  • Use Chrome or Edge. Firefox and Safari cannot send firmware.
